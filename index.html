LlamaBox Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Introduction

LlamaBox is a private, on-device AI assistant. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

LlamaBox does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

All AI processing happens locally on your device. Your conversations, prompts, and model files never leave your phone.

What Data We Access

  • Storage: To import and manage GGUF model files you choose to download or import.
  • Microphone: Only when you explicitly enable voice input. Audio is processed locally for speech-to-text and is never uploaded.
  • Notifications: For download progress when fetching models from the internet.

Third-Party Services

LlamaBox may connect to HuggingFace or other public model repositories only when you explicitly choose to download a model. No personal data is shared during these downloads.

Data Storage

All chat history and settings are stored locally in an SQLite database on your device. You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.

Children's Privacy

LlamaBox is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app.
